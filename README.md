# VRChat Group Auto Moderation

VRChat Group Auto Moderation is a colorful Windows moderation assistant built for VRChat group owners, staff members, and trusted moderators.

The app gives staff a clear view of what is happening inside their group instances through live player tracking, world information, moderation logs, and visual status indicators. Color-coded logs help staff quickly distinguish normal activity, warnings, detected threats, and completed moderation actions without searching through confusing raw data.

## Moderation and Safety Tools

The application provides a broad spectrum of moderation tools:

- Group, user, and avatar blacklists
- Up to 100 blacklisted groups per account
- Flagged-word warnings for usernames, bios, and statuses
- Staff group recognition to reduce accidental moderation
- Staff activity alerts for warnings, kicks, bans, and unbans
- Suspicious activity and raid detection
- Udon exploit and crash detection
- Force-teleport detection when supported by the world
- Moderation history with player names, reasons, timestamps, and color-coded events

Flagged words are warning-only and do not automatically ban users, allowing staff to review the situation before taking action.

## World and Player Information

Staff can view important session details in one place:

- Current world name, author, capacity, description, dates, and tags
- Live player list
- Player account information
- Current avatars
- Group and instance activity
- Recent moderation actions

Quick controls make it easy to review a player’s current avatar or add a group to the blacklist when needed.

## Designed for Staff

The application is designed to help moderators move from awareness to action quickly. Normal activity, warnings, suspicious behavior, and completed actions are presented with distinct visual colors so staff can scan the logs and understand the situation at a glance.

The bright, colorful interface is intended to make long moderation sessions easier to read while keeping important safety information visible.

## Responsible Moderation

VRChat Group Auto Moderation supports human decision-making rather than replacing staff judgment. Group owners can configure their own rules, review alerts, and decide how their community responds.

The application:

- Does not modify the VRChat client
- Does not bypass access controls
- Does not interfere with VRChat platform moderation
- Uses permitted group APIs and intentionally exposed world-side moderation events
- Performs world moderation actions only when supported by the world creator
- Keeps moderation information available for review and accountability

The goal is to give VRChat group staff better visibility, faster warnings, and a colorful but organized set of tools for protecting their communities.
